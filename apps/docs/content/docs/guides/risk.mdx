## Key Metrics

### Value at Risk (VaR)
The maximum expected loss over a given time horizon at a given confidence level.

- **VaR 95%** — You expect to lose more than this only 5% of the time
- **VaR 99%** — Worst-case scenario for 99% of outcomes

### Sharpe Ratio
Measures risk-adjusted return. A Sharpe ratio above 1.0 is good; above 2.0 is excellent.

### Maximum Drawdown
The largest peak-to-trough decline in portfolio value. Understanding your historical worst-case is essential for position sizing.

### Beta
Measures your portfolio's sensitivity to a benchmark (default: S&P 500).
- Beta = 1.0: Moves with the market
- Beta > 1.0: More volatile than the market
- Beta < 1.0: Less volatile than the market

### Correlation Matrix
Shows how your positions move relative to each other. High correlations mean less diversification benefit.
