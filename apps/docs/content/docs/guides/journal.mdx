## Why Keep a Journal?

The investment journal is OpenMoney's unique feature. It treats every investment decision as a **testable hypothesis**. By logging your reasoning and tracking outcomes, you can measure how calibrated your predictions are.

## Creating an Entry

1. Navigate to **Journal**
2. Click **New Prediction**
3. Fill in:
   - **Title** — A brief description of your thesis
   - **Ticker** — Optional, link to a security
   - **Direction** — Bullish, Bearish, or Neutral
   - **Thesis** — Your reasoning in detail
   - **Confidence** — How sure you are (50–99%)
   - **Timeframe** — Short, Medium, or Long term

## Tracking Outcomes

When a prediction resolves:
1. Open the journal entry
2. Click **Add Outcome**
3. Select: Correct, Incorrect, Too Early, or Too Late
4. Add notes on what you learned

## Accuracy Dashboard

Your prediction accuracy is measured with:
- **Calibration Curve** — Were you calibrated at each confidence level?
- **Brier Score** — Overall accuracy metric
- **Accuracy by Confidence Bracket** — Breakdown by your stated confidence
